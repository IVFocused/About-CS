The first career i chose was Game Developer,

A Game Developer is someone who creates video games as a designer, programmer, or artist.

A Game Developer usually makes around 97,000$ as an annual salary.

A bachelor's degree in computer science is required for a Game Developer.

I chose this job because i always have something to complain about when playing video games, but if make it myself i can edit every part and try to make it perfect.

The second career i chose was Story Board Artist.

A story board artist is someone who creates a series of drawings showing the flow of a movie/ad.

A story board artist makes around 55,000-95,000$ as an annual salary.

A story board artist usually requires a bachelor's degree in film.

I chose this career because i find it incredibly interesting to plan out things, specifically movies.

The last career i chose was Interactive Designer.

An interactive designer is someone who creates websites that encourage user participation.

A interactive designer makes around 99,520$ as an annual salary.

An interactive designer usually requires good knowledge of HTML and other coding languages, as well as knowing how to use editing programs like photo shop to make the assets.

I chose this career because i always wanted to make a website, the fact that i can edit it however i want is the best part. And making websites for other people sounds fairly easy.
