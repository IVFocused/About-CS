Digital Divide

Q:What technology is considered for this issue?

A:The digital divide is a worldwide technology issue. Digital divide is the gap between those with access to technology and those without direct access to technology. People living in rural countries or people living in low income households may have a hard time ontainining necessary technology for communication and or learning. This issue causes a big difference between those with technology an those without and those without technology. People with easy access to tech may have a better chance at college for example.

Q:Why is this an ethical issue?

A:This is an ethical issue because people in rural or unsupported countries cannot get tech easily. Some people might have no technology access at all. Due to the fact some people cannot use or get technology at all, they cannot get advanced education such as college.

Q:Who does it impact the most?

A:Small groups of people living in mountains, low income households, and or unsupported rural countries.
